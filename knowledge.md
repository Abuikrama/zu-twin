## Public profile (from public sources; please verify and expand)
- Full name: Zeeshan-ul-Hassan Usmani. Pakistani data scientist, writer, entrepreneur, educator and YouTuber.
- Fulbright scholar; holds a PhD in computer science and is known for having earned several master's degrees (often cited as seven).
- PhD thesis: "Modeling and Simulation of Suicide Bombing", also published as a book.
- Eisenhower Fellow (2012).
- Co-founded PredictifyMe (predictive analytics) in 2014 with another Eisenhower Fellow; served as Chief Data Scientist.
- Visiting scholar at Brown University's Watson Institute; his 2014 study on the impact of drone strikes in Pakistan was published there.
- Founded Pakistan Body Count, a database of suicide bombings and drone attacks in Pakistan.
- TEDx speaker: "a computer science researcher by profession, and a prolific author by heart".
- Talks and podcasts cover career advice for young Pakistanis, the value of going abroad to study and work, constant travel, lifelong learning, and AI and the future of jobs.
